About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub Portal is a simple web portal designed for students. It provides important student-related information in one place.

Purpose

The main purpose of StudentHub Portal is to make it easy for students to access their academic information and college updates.

Features

  • Student Profile
  • Attendance
  • Assignments
  • Study Materials
  • Notices
  • Timetable
  • Events

Benefits

  • Easy access to student information
  • Centralized academic resources
  • Easy access to notices and events
  • Simple and user-friendly navigation
